# Module 112 — The CAO Operating Model

> Closing module of the Chief AI Officer
> foundational track. Ties together the
> operating-model choice from
> [`mod-101`](../mod-101-foundations/README.md),
> the structural discipline accumulated across
> [`mod-104`](../mod-104-model-risk-management/README.md)
> through
> [`mod-110`](../mod-110-incident-response/README.md),
> and the board-facing posture from
> [`mod-111`](../mod-111-board-reporting/README.md)
> — into a coherent view of *what running the
> function actually looks like* over a
> multi-year horizon.

## What you will leave with

After working through this module you should
be able to:

1. Make the structural operating-model choices
   a CAO function rests on — centralised vs
   federated vs hub-and-spoke — with awareness
   of the consequences each choice carries
   forward.
2. Author a year-1 operating plan that
   survives the realities of standing up a
   new function.
3. Identify what year 2 should look like and
   how the program transitions from
   foundation-laying to consolidation.
4. Recognise what year-3 maturity looks like
   — and the warning signs the program is
   stalling at year-2.
5. Operate the CAO calendar deliberately
   rather than reactively, including the
   practice patterns that separate effective
   CAOs from exhausted ones.
6. Build a talent and succession strategy
   that doesn't leave the program dependent
   on a single load-bearing executive.
7. Coordinate the enterprise AI budget
   envelope with the CFO and cross-functional
   CxO peers at CEO / board scope.

## A note on this being the closing module

The other 11 modules each owned a specific
domain. This module owns the *integration* —
how the domains fit together in practice. It
is also the most personal module: how the CAO
operates day-to-day, week-to-week,
year-to-year is shaped by personal choices
alongside structural ones. The chapters
acknowledge this; the exercises do not pretend
there is one right answer.

## How this module fits

[`mod-101`](../mod-101-foundations/README.md)
Chapter 6 introduced the three operating-model
patterns; this module revisits the choice
from the perspective of a CAO who has now
seen what each produces downstream. The
year-1/2/3 arc (Chapters 2-4) places the
disciplines from the preceding modules into
a temporal sequence. The calendar (Chapter 5)
is how the CAO allocates attention across
all of them. Talent and succession
(Chapter 6) and budget coordination
(Chapter 7) are the resource and
accountability substrate the function
depends on.

Downstream from this module are the capstone
projects and sector-specific deep-dives
noted in `resources.md`.
