# Exercise 01 — Design a Year-1 Operating Plan

**Estimated time**: 3 hours
**Deliverable**: Year-1 operating plan (≤ 4 pages)

---

## The scenario

You are the newly-appointed CAO at **Northfield
Mutual** (continuity from
[`mod-101`](../../mod-101-foundations/README.md)
Exercise 02). Your appointment was triggered
by the state insurance regulator's inquiry
about AI governance posture. You have 12
months. You need to produce an operating plan
to walk into your first AI Risk Council
meeting.

## Your assignment

Produce a year-1 operating plan with:

### Section 1 — Operating model (≤ ½ page)

The structural choice per Chapter 1 —
centralised, federated, or hub-and-spoke —
with reasoning. For Northfield's mid-market
US P&C insurer profile. Address at least
three of the five contextual factors from
Chapter 1 §1.2.

### Section 2 — Quarter-by-quarter milestones (≤ 1½ pages)

Per Chapter 2 §2.1, the six workstreams
year 1 should accomplish, distributed across
Q1-Q4 with specific milestones per quarter.

| Quarter | Milestone | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | ... | ... |
| Q2 | ... | ... |
| Q3 | ... | ... |
| Q4 | ... | ... |

Milestones should be concrete enough that an
AI Risk Council member reading the plan can
tell at the end of each quarter whether the
milestone was hit.

### Section 3 — Hiring sequence (≤ ½ page)

The 4-5 roles to hire in year 1, in sequence,
with timing per Chapter 6 §6.2. Address the
specific §6.2.1 sequencing errors — show that
you have not committed any of them.

### Section 4 — What year 1 explicitly will not do (≤ ½ page)

Per Chapter 2 §2.2 — things year 1 will
defer to year 2 with reasoning. At least
three specific items, each with a
one-sentence defence of the deferral.

### Section 5 — Mid-year-1 inflection point preparation (≤ ¼ page)

How you will handle the Chapter 2 §2.3
inflection point when the foundation work
feels invisible. What specifically will you
show in your Q2 and Q3 board reports to
communicate foundation progress as the
deliverable?

### Section 6 — Year-1 self-assessment criteria (≤ ¼ page)

What "year 1 succeeded" means specifically.
The criteria you commit to upfront. Reference
Chapter 2 §2.4 markers and the broader
self-assessment pattern from
[`mod-111`](../../mod-111-board-reporting/README.md)
Chapter 6.

### Section 7 — Budget posture (≤ ½ page)

Year-1 budget framing per Chapter 7 §7.4.
Tie investment to risk reduction, phase the
budget against the foundation arc, and name
the cost of not investing in at least one
specific place. Where relevant, reference
the three budgets from Chapter 7 §7.1 —
function budget, enterprise envelope, risk
reserve.

## Constraints

- The operating-model choice must be
  **defended** against the Chapter 1 §1.2
  factors.
- Quarterly milestones must be **specific** —
  not "set up the program" but "AI Risk
  Council charter adopted; first meeting
  held".
- The "what year 1 will not do" section must
  include **at least three** specific items —
  programs that try to do everything in year
  1 fail predictably.
- The hiring sequence must respect Chapter 6
  §6.2 — the AI Risk Lead is hire #1; the
  other foundation roles follow in defensible
  order.
- The budget posture must distinguish between
  the CAO function budget and the enterprise
  AI envelope (Chapter 7 §7.1) — a plan that
  treats them as one bucket is not using
  the discipline the chapter teaches.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Operating model — defended against §1.2 factors | 15% |
| Quarterly milestones — specific, phased | 25% |
| Hiring sequence per §6.2 | 15% |
| What year 1 will not do — at least three | 15% |
| Inflection point preparation | 10% |
| Self-assessment criteria | 10% |
| Budget posture — three-budget distinction and §7.4 framing | 10% |
