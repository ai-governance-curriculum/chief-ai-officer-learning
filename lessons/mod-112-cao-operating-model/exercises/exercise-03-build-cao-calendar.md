# Exercise 03 — Build a CAO Calendar

**Estimated time**: 2 hours
**Deliverable**: Quarterly + annual calendar (≤ 2 pages)

---

## The scenario

You are the CAO at any of the fictional
institutions used in the track. The Board
Risk Committee chair has noted — casually,
but pointedly — that your calendar appears
entirely reactive. You decide to author
your own operating calendar, the one
Chapter 5 §5.1 describes: deliberate, not
reactive.

## Your assignment

Produce two artifacts.

### Artifact 1 — The recurring rhythm (≤ 1 page)

Per Chapter 5 §5.1, the calendar across
cadences:

| Cadence | Day / time | Activity | Owner | Specific output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily | 09:00, 15 min | Standup with AI Risk Lead | AI Risk Lead | Overnight incidents / escalations reviewed |
| Weekly | Monday 09:30 | Weekly bias / drift / abuse monitoring review | AI Risk Lead | Signals surfaced; decisions queued for AIRB |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

Aim for ~15 recurring items across daily,
weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, and
annually. Each item must have a specific
output (per Chapter 5 §5.1); items without
specific outputs are candidates for
elimination, not inclusion.

### Artifact 2 — Time protection and crisis discipline (≤ 1 page)

Per Chapter 5 §5.2, the protected time
blocks. For each of the four:

- **Strategic thinking** — when, how often,
  the artifact it produces.
- **Direct report development** — the 1:1
  cadence, protection discipline.
- **Stakeholder relationship-building** —
  which peers, which cadence.
- **External engagement** — which external
  relationships, which cadence.

For each, specify (per Chapter 5 §5.2.5):

- The block (time, duration, frequency).
- The conditions under which it can be
  interrupted (per §5.2.5, few).
- The recovery pattern after disruption
  (blocks that aren't recovered no longer
  exist).

Plus a **crisis discipline statement** per
Chapter 5 §5.4:

- Named fire mode trigger.
- The end-date discipline.
- The specific calendar reset half-day
  pattern (within 2 weeks of resolution).

## Constraints

- The recurring rhythm must include all six
  Chapter 5 §5.1 cadences (daily through
  annually).
- At least 3 of the items must be
  **delegated** — the CAO is not the owner,
  per the Chapter 5 §5.3 delegation discipline
  (AI Risk Lead handles operational response;
  Policy Lead owns standards; Evidence Lead
  owns the ledger; Regulatory Engagement Lead
  owns routine regulator interface).
- The time protection must include at least
  one **inviolable** block — a block
  interruptible only by a materiality-
  threshold incident or a direct board
  request, per §5.2.1 or §5.2.2.
- The crisis discipline must include a
  specific **reset pattern** with timing
  (within 2 weeks).
- Each recurring item has a stated
  **specific output**. Items without one
  are not included.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
