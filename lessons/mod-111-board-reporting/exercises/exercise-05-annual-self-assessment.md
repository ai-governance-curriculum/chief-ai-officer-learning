# Exercise 05 — Annual Program Self-Assessment

**Estimated time**: 3 hours
**Deliverable**: Self-assessment + recommendations (≤ 8 pages, including portfolio ROI reconciliation)

---

## The scenario

You are the CAO at **Halverston Capital** (the
same organisation as Exercise 01). End of
your first full year as CAO. The annual
self-assessment is due. The Audit Committee
will read it; the AI Risk Council will use
it as input to year 2 planning; the CFO
will consume the portfolio reconciliation
section.

The trailing year included:

- Operationalising the AI Risk Council and
  the AI Review Board.
- Implementing the trust architecture per
  the
  [`mod-106`](../../mod-106-trust-architecture/README.md)
  Exercise 05 partner pattern.
- The first cross-LOB tabletop exercise
  (per
  [`mod-110`](../../mod-110-incident-response/README.md)
  Exercise 03).
- One material incident (a near-miss on a
  bias threshold in onboarding fraud
  screening, avoided via the early-
  detection cadence; a post-incident review
  was conducted even though no customer
  harm occurred).
- The ISO 42001 certification process (in
  progress; certification body engaged, gap
  assessment complete, remediation
  underway).
- A material vendor change in the LLM
  underlying wealth advisory (pre-notified
  by vendor; swap executed per Chapter 7
  §7.2 inventory with risk-tier re-review).
- Hiring 6 net new roles in the CAO
  function against a plan of 8 (two roles
  deferred to year 2 due to market
  compensation pressure on the independent
  validation function).
- Portfolio operating effectiveness —
  governance costs attributed; the ROI
  reporting pattern established jointly
  with the CFO using the realised-incident
  risk-adjustment pattern (Chapter 7 §7.5.1).

## Your assignment

Produce a self-assessment following
[Chapter 6 §6.3](../06-annual-self-assessment.md#63-the-structure):

### Executive summary (≤ 1 page)

The three substantive findings and the three
substantive recommendations. Chapter 6 §6.2
honesty test applies — would a peer CAO at
a comparable institution judge this credible?

### Section 1 — Program maturity (≤ 1 page)

Rating against NIST AI RMF across the four
functions (GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE). For
each: current level, specific evidence, gap
to next level. Chapter 6 §6.1.1.

### Section 2 — Operating effectiveness (≤ 1 page)

Chapter 6 §6.1.2 — are the controls
operating? What does the evidence show?
Specific incidents (the near-miss); specific
exercises (the tabletop). Honest finding on
what worked and what did not.

### Section 3 — Coverage gaps (≤ ¾ page)

Chapter 6 §6.1.3 — the top 3 to 5 gaps with
specific recommendations.

### Section 4 — Investment posture (≤ ¾ page)

Chapter 6 §6.1.4 — where the program
invested; where it under-invested; year-ahead
investment focus. Specific numbers.

### Section 5 — Leadership and talent (≤ ½ page)

Chapter 6 §6.1.5 — hiring outcomes (6 of 8
roles); capability gaps remaining (the two
deferred independent validation roles);
talent risks (key-person dependencies, market
competition for AI governance talent).

### Section 6 — Cultural integration (≤ ½ page)

Chapter 6 §6.1.6 — integrated or operating
in parallel? Evidence either way.

### Section 7 — Portfolio reconciliation (≤ ¾ page)

Not in Chapter 6's base structure — adds the
Chapter 7 portfolio view at annual altitude:

- Portfolio summary (spend, gross returns,
  risk-adjusted returns, year-over-year
  change).
- Methodology disclosure (attribution
  pattern, risk-adjustment pattern).
- Reconciliation to the CFO's financial
  record — any material differences
  disclosed and explained.
- Concentration risks called out —
  especially after the wealth-advisory
  vendor swap.

### Section 8 — Specific recommendations (≤ ½ page)

A prioritised year-2 action list — specific
owner, specific timeline, specific tracking.
Chapter 6 §6.4.2 applies.

## Constraints

- The assessment must be **honest**. The
  Chapter 6 §6.2 honesty test: would the CAO
  be comfortable showing this to a peer CAO
  at a comparable institution who would give
  an honest read?
- Each section must include **specific
  evidence**, not assertions.
- At least one section must surface a
  **substantive** finding — something
  meaningful, not a trivial improvement.
- The recommendations must be **specific**
  — same discipline as mod-110 post-incident
  review recommendations (owner, timeline,
  tracking).
- The CAO function's underperformance, if
  any, must be honestly named — the two
  deferred independent-validation roles are
  the obvious candidate. Self-assessments
  that name only successes fail the honesty
  test.
- The portfolio reconciliation (§7) must
  disclose the risk-adjustment pattern used
  and reconcile to the CFO's record. Chapter
  7 §7.6 applies.
- The assessment must avoid the three
  Chapter 6 §6.6 failure modes — marketing
  self-assessment, compliance self-
  assessment, parallel self-assessment —
  explicitly.
