# Module 111 — Resources

Annotated reading list for Board Reporting
and Risk Appetite. Standards-first.

## Tier 1 — Authoritative primary sources

| Source | Why it matters | Chapters anchored |
|---|---|---|
| [COSO ERM — Enterprise Risk Management framework (2017) + Compliance Risk Management Applying the COSO ERM Framework (2020)](https://www.coso.org/guidance-erm) | The appetite framework Chapter 2 operationalises for AI | 2 |
| [NIST AI RMF 1.0 (AI 100-1)](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) | The GOVERN function categories and the maturity vocabulary the self-assessment uses | 2, 6 |
| [NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1)](https://airc.nist.gov/AI_RMF_Knowledge_Base/Playbook) | GenAI-specific GOVERN subcategories | 2, 6 |
| [ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI Management System](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html) | §5.3 top management role, §6.1 risk / opportunity, §9.3 management review — the ISO analogue of the annual self-assessment | 2, 6 |
| [EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) | Art. 73 serious-incident notification; Art. 51 systemic-risk thresholds for GPAI | 4 |

| Source | Sector | Relevance |
|---|---|---|

| [CFPB Circular 2022-03 — Adverse-action notification requirements in connection with credit decisions based on complex algorithms](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/circulars/circular-2022-03-adverse-action-notification-requirements-in-connection-with-credit-decisions-based-on-complex-algorithms/) | US consumer credit | Reg B §1002.9 specificity — relevant to Exercise 04 context |
| [NAIC Model Bulletin — Use of AI Systems by Insurers (2023)](https://content.naic.org/) | US insurance | State-level AI governance expectations for insurers |
