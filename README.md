Enterprise AI strategy, portfolio P&L, board-level AI risk oversight, and CEO-scope external positioning for the Chief AI Officer / Chief Data & AI Officer / Group Head of AI / VP-of-AI Transformation seat.

> **Status**: planned. Phase 1 research (26 in-window postings, 2026-09-08 live pass) is complete. See [`CURRICULUM.md`](CURRICULUM.md) for the module and project plan, [`JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md`](JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md) for the requirements-to-coverage map, and [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json) for the machine-readable plan.

## What this track owns

The CEO-scope AI agenda at **level 70** — enterprise AI strategy, AI portfolio P&L attribution, board-visible AI ROI reporting, external positioning at CEO / board / press / voluntary-code scope, the enterprise AI budget envelope, AI-first workforce transformation, AI investment-case authoring to CFO and board, AI Governance Board / federal CAIO Council leadership under OMB M-25-21, enterprise build-vs-buy at portfolio scope, and cross-functional partnership across CFO / CIO / CTO / CDO / CHRO / GC / CRO / CISO / CMO / CPO.

Defers *down* to [`head-of-ai-governance-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/head-of-ai-governance-learning) (level 60) for leadership execution of the governance function, and *out of scope* to the peer CxOs (GC, CPO, CRO, CCO, CAE, CFO) for their enterprise-wide mandates. See [`CURRICULUM.md`](CURRICULUM.md) for the full ownership rule.

Total planned commitment: **~329 hours** — 12 modules (217h) + 5 capstone projects (112h).
